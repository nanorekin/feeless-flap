How to play

Goal

You are an XNO coin on a mission to avoid fees. Tap, click or press Space to flap upwards. Gravity pulls you back down. Fly through the gaps in the red fee gates without touching a gate, the ground or the very top of the screen's flight limit.

Scoring

Every gate you pass scores one point. The course speeds up and the gaps shrink a little as you go, up to a limit.

Medals

Bronze at 10 gates, Silver at 25, Gold at 50 and Diamond at 100.

Daily course

The daily course has the same gate layout for everyone and changes at 00:00 UTC. Pass 15 gates to clear it and keep your daily streak. You can retry as often as you like.

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks extra coin skins. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
